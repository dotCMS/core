## Where panels toast

A panel resolves PrimeNG `MessageService` through the injector it was opened with, never through where the outlet sits: the outlet creates the content under `Injector.create({ parent: config.injector })` with that injector's environment injector (`dot-side-panel-content.directive.ts`). So a panel must be opened with an injector under a component that provides `MessageService` and renders a `<p-toast />`; the root `Injector` a root service gets from `inject(Injector)` reaches neither. `DotSidePanelHostComponent` (`apps/dotcms-ui/src/app/view/components/dot-side-panel-host/`) renders `<dot-side-panel-outlet />` and a `<p-toast />`, provides PrimeNG `MessageService`, and registers its injector with `DotCustomEventHandlerService`, so the panels the legacy JSP events open toast there. It is a sibling of the router outlet, not an ancestor: a `MessageService` on `MainComponentLegacyComponent` shadowed the one UVE provides at route level and every UVE toast rendered twice. Every other opener provides its own `MessageService` and renders its own `<p-toast />`: Content Drive, the Query Tool, UVE (route level), the Pages portlet, both Publishing Queue dialogs and the deep-link component.

## Escape over a dialog (US3 AC 3, SC-007)

A panel opened over a dialog (the Publishing Queue's asset list and bundle selection, the Workflow Tasks detail) closes on the first Escape, and the dialog on the second. The panel claims Escape through the shortcut registry, whose one document listener stays in the bubble phase so anything inside the panel that handles Escape on its own element still gets it first. When the front panel asks to close, its handler calls `stopImmediatePropagation()`: a PrimeNG dialog listens to the document's keydown itself and closes when it is the top of `ZIndexUtils`, and the panel's close destroys the drawer and drops its entry, so a dialog listener later in the same event found itself on top and closed too. With an overlay above the panel, the panel leaves the event alone, since that overlay's own listener must hear it. The iframe dialog (`DotIframeDialogComponent`, the Workflow Tasks detail) gets keys from inside its iframe, where the page's listeners never hear them: while a layer sits above it, it does not focus its iframe on load (the detail reloads behind the panel after each save) and hands an Escape from inside the iframe to the page (a new `keydown` on the document) instead of closing.

## The panel in the URL (FR-044 to FR-050)

Every surface in the table except UVE writes the open panel to its URL by passing `url: {}` to the opener (opener-api.md). The opener owns the params (`editContent` + `editContentLang`, `createContent`, any `extraParams` the surface passes, `editCode`), the history entry per open, the in-place rewrites on language switch, a create's first save, a new translation's first save and close, the Back listener that routes through `requestClose()`, and the stack (the URL names the front panel). A panel the opener does not open (Content Drive's Edit Code) registers with `DotPanelUrlStateService` itself. A surface does three things: passes the option, reads `readPanelUrlIntent(route.snapshot.queryParams)` once on init and opens what it names (an edit by `identifier` and `languageId`, which the opener resolves to the working version), and refuses a link the author cannot have meant, the intent `refused` (a panel param of the wrong shape, or more than one panel), with `DotPanelUrlStateService.refuse(extraKeys)`: it opens nothing, shows the standard not-found error the opener shows for content that does not exist, so a malformed link and a missing one read the same, and clears the params (Content Drive passes its three folder dialog keys, the legacy portlets `folder`) (T172). Before T172 a malformed value read as absent and a surface opened nothing in silence, leaving the junk in the address bar. The legacy portlets and Pages read the router's `queryParams` on every navigation of the app's own instead of once, since a deep link reaches them by a router navigation after they were built; they skip a navigation the browser's Back or Forward started (`router.currentNavigation()?.trigger === 'popstate'`), which is the service's and may name a panel still open, and deduplicate the rest with `panelUrlIntentKey`. `readPanelUrlIntent` is in `@dotcms/utils`; the admin app may not import `@dotcms/edit-content` statically (it loads it on demand), so its screens refuse through `refusePanelUrl(injector, extraKeys)` beside `openEditContentPanel`. A surface that writes params of its own builds the URL from `Location.path(true)` and changes only its own keys, since the router never saw what the service wrote (Content Drive's and the Query Tool's `updateQueryParamsEffect`). Content Drive and the Query Tool delete their own implementations (`#syncEditContentParam`, the panel half of the `Location.subscribe` blocks, `resolveContentDriveUrlIntent`'s edit and create kinds, which now come from `readPanelUrlIntent`; its folder-dialog kinds stay), and their own identifier lookups: both hand the opener `{ mode: 'edit', identifier, languageId }` (Content Drive's `languageId` is the one it prefers, the URL's, else its filter's, else the default), and the opener finds the version, falls back to any language and shows the standard error (FR-050). Content Drive then knows a panel it opened that way by its identifier (`EditContentIdentity.identifier`), as it never sees the inode the opener found. The deep-link component opens nothing: it redirects to the parent portlet's URL with the panel params (`router.navigate`, `replaceUrl: true`, `queryParamsHandling: 'merge'`), so the path form never stays in history and the link is then an ordinary URL that names a panel. The URL is written with `Location.go` and `Location.replaceState`, never `router.navigate`: the legacy iframe reloads on every `queryParams` emission and `reuseRoute: false` portlets are recreated on navigation.

A panel stacked on a written panel is written too, and one stacked on a panel UVE opened is not: every stacked panel, from either editor, is opened by the opener's `openContent` handler, which passes `url: {}` only when the panel underneath had it (opener-api.md step 6). The new editor's `OverlayEditContentHost` hands what its fields stack to its panel's `openContent` output instead of opening it (open-content-from-fields.md).

Leaving a surface closes its panels: when a router navigation ends on a path other than the one a written panel was opened on (Back, the menu, a link inside a legacy portlet's frame), `DotPanelUrlStateService` closes every such panel, front first, without its guard, and writes nothing to the other screen's URL. Most panels would go with their surface's injector anyway; the legacy portlets' panels are opened under the app-level `DotSidePanelHostComponent` and outlived it until this (opener-api.md, "Leaving the surface").

Back with a panel open is answered by the router too: dotcms-ui sets `onSameUrlNavigation: 'reload'`, so it navigates on every Back. A surface whose route has `data: { reuseRoute: false }` is rebuilt by that navigation and loses its panel, so it drops the flag from its route, as the Query Tool and the Publishing Queue (`publishing-queue-beta`) did; `DotCustomReuseStrategyService` stays as it is, because UVE (`/edit-page`) reads its page from the URL once and depends on a query-only navigation rebuilding it (browser Back between pages, the vanity-URL redirect). Content Drive's and Pages' routes have no flag; Pages' `:asset` child no longer carries one either, since it redirects at once, and neither does `/c/:id/:asset`, whose Workflow Tasks route keeps a panel in its URL (the components on it follow their params).

Accepted trade-off: closing the front of two stacked panels with its X rewrites its history entry to name the panel underneath, so two consecutive entries name the same panel and the next Back is a visible no-op (the URL already names the panel in front); a second Back reaches that panel's guard. A `history.back()` on close would remove the duplicate but race the service's own `popstate` listener, which would read that Back as the author's and ask the panel underneath to close.

Pre-existing, not changed here: when a URL-mapped page's content is saved with a new URL, UVE renders the new page twice. The panel's `saved` loads it (`pageLoad({ url })`, the first render); `pageLoad` calls `markPageLoading()`, which sets `isClientReady: false`, and when the new page's iframe posts `CLIENT_READY`, `DotUveActionsHandlerService` answers it with `pageReload()` because the client is not marked ready (the second render). Every `pageLoad` that changes the page does the same. Main has the same handshake (`withPageApi`'s `pageLoad` → `markPageLoading()`, and the `CLIENT_READY` handler's `pageReload()` when the client is not ready), so this is the UVE's page-load behaviour, not the panel's. Found by QA of layer 8 (E2c); the two renders were traced to those two callers with CDP initiator stacks.
