## Where panels toast

A panel resolves PrimeNG `MessageService` through the injector it was opened with, never through where the outlet sits: the outlet creates the content under `Injector.create({ parent: config.injector })` with that injector's environment injector (`dot-side-panel-content.directive.ts`). So a panel must be opened with an injector under a component that provides `MessageService` and renders a `<p-toast />`; the root `Injector` a root service gets from `inject(Injector)` reaches neither. `DotSidePanelHostComponent` (`apps/dotcms-ui/src/app/view/components/dot-side-panel-host/`) renders `<dot-side-panel-outlet />` and a `<p-toast />`, provides PrimeNG `MessageService`, and registers its injector with `DotCustomEventHandlerService`, so the panels the legacy JSP events open toast there. It is a sibling of the router outlet, not an ancestor: a `MessageService` on `MainComponentLegacyComponent` shadowed the one UVE provides at route level and every UVE toast rendered twice. Every other opener provides its own `MessageService` and renders its own `<p-toast />`: Content Drive, the Query Tool, UVE (route level), the Pages portlet, both Publishing Queue dialogs and the deep-link component.

## The panel in the URL (FR-044 to FR-050)

Every surface in the table except UVE writes the open panel to its URL by passing `url: {}` to the opener (opener-api.md). The opener owns the params (`editContent` + `editContentLang`, `createContent`, any `extraParams` the surface passes, `editCode`), the history entry per open, the in-place rewrites on language switch, a create's first save, a new translation's first save and close, the Back listener that routes through `requestClose()`, and the stack (the URL names the front panel). A panel the opener does not open (Content Drive's Edit Code) registers with `DotPanelUrlStateService` itself. A surface does three things: passes the option, reads `readPanelUrlIntent(route.snapshot.queryParams)` once on init and opens what it names (an edit by `identifier` and `languageId`, which the opener resolves to the working version), and clears the params when the intent is `conflict`, with `DotPanelUrlStateService.clear(extraKeys)` (Content Drive passes its three folder dialog keys, the legacy portlets `folder`). The legacy portlets and Pages read the router's `queryParams` on every change instead of once, deduplicated by value, since a deep link reaches them by a router navigation after they were built. `readPanelUrlIntent` is in `@dotcms/utils`; the admin app may not import `@dotcms/edit-content` statically (it loads it on demand), so its screens clear through `clearPanelUrl(injector, extraKeys)` beside `openEditContentPanel`. A surface that writes params of its own builds the URL from `Location.path(true)` and changes only its own keys, since the router never saw what the service wrote (Content Drive's `updateQueryParamsEffect`). Content Drive and the Query Tool delete their own implementations (`#syncEditContentParam`, the panel half of the `Location.subscribe` blocks, `resolveContentDriveUrlIntent`'s edit and create kinds, which now come from `readPanelUrlIntent`; its folder-dialog kinds stay). The deep-link component opens nothing: it redirects to the parent portlet's URL with the panel params (`router.navigate`, `replaceUrl: true`, `queryParamsHandling: 'merge'`), so the path form never stays in history and the link is then an ordinary URL that names a panel. The URL is written with `Location.go` and `Location.replaceState`, never `router.navigate`: the legacy iframe reloads on every `queryParams` emission and `reuseRoute: false` portlets are recreated on navigation.

Back with a panel open is answered by the router too: dotcms-ui sets `onSameUrlNavigation: 'reload'`, so it navigates on every Back. A surface whose route has `data: { reuseRoute: false }` is rebuilt by that navigation and loses its panel, so it drops the flag from its route, as the Query Tool and the Publishing Queue (`publishing-queue-beta`) did; `DotCustomReuseStrategyService` stays as it is, because UVE (`/edit-page`) reads its page from the URL once and depends on a query-only navigation rebuilding it (browser Back between pages, the vanity-URL redirect). Content Drive's and Pages' routes have no flag; Pages' `:asset` child no longer carries one either, since it redirects at once.
